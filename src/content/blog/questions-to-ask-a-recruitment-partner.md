Recruitment partners differ widely in what they offer and how they work. Asking a few direct questions at the start helps you choose one whose approach fits your business.

## What exactly is included?

Ask for a clear description of the services provided: sourcing, screening, evaluation, coordination, or some combination. Be clear about which steps remain your responsibility.

## How are candidates assessed?

Find out what criteria are used and who applies them. A structured assessment against your requirements is more useful than a general impression of a candidate.

## How will we communicate?

Understand who your contact will be, how often you will receive updates and how problems are raised. Clear communication matters most when something does not go to plan.

## What can and cannot be promised?

Be cautious of any partner who guarantees specific outcomes without qualification. Recruitment depends on market conditions, candidate decisions and administrative steps that no one fully controls. An honest partner explains the factors involved.

## How are legal and regulatory requirements handled?

Establish who is responsible for what. Employers should confirm requirements that apply to them with qualified advisers, and a responsible recruitment partner will flag issues early rather than leaving them to the end.

## What do you need from us?

A good partner will tell you what information, decisions and response times they need from you. Recruitment is a joint effort, and understanding your own responsibilities helps the process run smoothly.
